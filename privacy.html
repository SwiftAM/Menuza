Menuza Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Menuza is designed to minimize the information it collects. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

Menu Data

When you use Menuza, you may create and store menu information, including categories, menu items, names, descriptions, prices, images, and other menu information that you choose to save.

Images

Menuza allows you to add images to your menu. Menuza does not provide a feature for uploading those images to an external server from the app.

Location

Menuza does not collect or store your location information.

Subscriptions

Menuza offers optional subscriptions that unlock premium features. Purchases and subscription management are handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

Menuza does not receive or store your full payment card information. Payment processing is handled by Apple.

iCloud

If you use iCloud synchronization, your Menuza data may be synchronized through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services so that your data can be available across supported Apple devices.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Precise location information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics data
  • Tracking information
  • Browsing history
  • Contact lists
  • Microphone recordings
  • Camera recordings

Menuza does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Creating and managing menus
  • Managing categories and menu items
  • Displaying menu content
  • Displaying images
  • Providing premium subscription features
  • Synchronizing supported data through iCloud

4. Data Storage

Menuza stores app data using Apple's SwiftData framework on your device. When iCloud synchronization is enabled, supported data may also be synchronized through Apple's CloudKit and iCloud services.

Menuza does not operate its own external database for storing your menu content.

5. Data Retention

Your Menuza data is retained while it remains stored in the app or in your iCloud-synchronized data.

You can delete your menu data from within the app.

6. Account Deletion

Menuza does not currently operate a traditional user-account system for its core functionality.

You can delete your stored Menuza data through the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Menuza is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

Menuza uses Apple's services to provide certain functionality, including App Store and StoreKit for subscriptions and purchases, and iCloud and CloudKit for data synchronization.

9. Tracking and Advertising

Menuza does not use third-party advertising networks, analytics, or tracking technologies.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored and processed by Menuza. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices

You control the information you store in Menuza by choosing what categories, menu items, images, and other content you add to the app.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
